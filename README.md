<h1> Purpose</h1>
<p> Fascinated and at the same time bewildered with the current political systems. I, a BA 2nd year student has created this website in hope of turning the tables and evolving a newer political system that'd be better than democracy. An audacious task indeed but I am going to journal it here, no matter if I find success or not. The project without any doubt is a lofty one but I am an optimist. Even if it's hyperfuturistic in its very nature, I am going to work on it. </p>

<h2> Novelty 1</h2>
<h3> Dialectical Pluralism in the age of AI and Large Language Models: Rethinking governance, consensus, and bias </h3>
<p> <b> <u> Abstract </u></b> </p>
<p> Democracy with its veil of liberty and legitimacy drawn from the masses, in its essence is lacking what it keeps boasting. In the largest democracy what we've got is nothing but majoritarianism, demagoguery, rise of parties on the popular waves of hypernationalism or propaganda driven governments. It has proved to be inefficient mode of governance on many grounds, it has blemished most of the principles that humanity carries, that is of Justice, liberty and equality. Surveillance, opinion suppressing through questionable methods is rampant. Therefore, the time has come once again to rethink governance and this is the most opportune moment of doing so.  </p>
<p> <b> <u> Dialectical Pluralism in the age of AI and Large Language Models: Rethinking governance, consesus, and bias </u></b> </p>
<p> Evolution through syntheses has hitherto been humanity's progress tool and it continues to be so. In the age of AI driven of the LLMs, data has become key and quickly processsing data an enormous achievement. Today's AI can easily take part in the human discourse and that too with high precision and logic. Other than syntheses, heterogeneity has been a core foundation that has led to the diversity that we see in today's world. We are living in a world with numerous truths which is still far from it. With the world coming together through globalisation and impacting adeversely the heterogenous structure with what we now term as soft power of culture, the need of preserving it from turning into homogenous structure is even more and evidently the democracy is not helping, impostition of views of those who are in power or majority has led to a crisis. And it is not just limited to 3rd world countries, even 1st world countries are facing the harsh punishment of incompetent leadership, whether it's Republican regime which bluntly negates the climate change and keeps their selfish capitalistic tendencies afront. The situation in 3rd world countries is even severe. The time has come to rethink governance, and the answer lies in the use of AI in pluralistic and dialectical decision making. And from this arise numerous other questions, such as, legitimacy of technocracy, transparency, ultimate goal of humanity, a question mark on political participationship of humans, are we going to be slaves of AI decisions, what if the model is controlled by the extremist forces, who is going to regulate it?   </p>
